# US & Canadian Census Field Coverage — Research (Living Document)

**Status:** RESEARCH COMPLETE — autonomous research session, 2026-08-15. All US federal (12 years, 1800-1830 deliberately deferred) and Canadian (10 years) census years researched on both Ancestry and FamilySearch. Not a finalized spec — field→GEDCOM mapping decisions here are PROPOSALS for joint review, not implemented anywhere yet (no code/YAML changed as part of this document). Next step: joint mapping-review session with the user (not autonomous).

**Purpose:** Extend `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-15-ancestry-index-panel-extraction-design.md` (which confirmed 1850/1860/1880/1920 live) to every remaining US and Canadian census year, via the same technique: Ancestry card catalog → real record → `imageviewer/api/record/index-panel-data` → real `fieldLabels`. Feeds GitHub issue #24.

**Method note:** Real family/place chosen per year based on where records actually exist for that year (Pembina, ND didn't exist as enumerated US territory before 1850 - pre-1850 years use a different, well-documented early-American place).

**SCOPE CORRECTION (user, mid-session): full research on BOTH Ancestry and FamilySearch for every year — not a light FS touch.** FS's orchestration API is confirmed self-describing/era-agnostic by design (no per-year branching needed in the parser itself), but the ACTUAL field vocabulary present per year still needs to be confirmed live per year, same rigor as Ancestry, since "self-describing" only means the PARSER doesn't need year-branching - it doesn't mean every year's real field set is already known.
